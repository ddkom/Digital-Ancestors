# Guardian

## "F*ck AI."

### The Archetype
The Guardian sees AI as a serious threat to people and the planet. For that reason, they say no to AI. Concerned about protecting their data and all the implications of using AI (such as the natural resource consumption and exploitative labour involved in operating), they choose to opt-out wherever they can. They do not use it for work nor leisure, and they're not alone. There's many like them, who see AI as extractive and continue to find ways to say no in an ever evolving tech landscape.

### Do This

#### In minutes
- Turn off AI training in your accounts: Meta ([AI at Meta objection form](https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/510058597920541) — a right in the EU/UK, a request elsewhere), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6278444) (Settings → Data Privacy → Data for Generative AI Improvement → off), Grok, TikTok

#### In an hour
- - If you have your own website turn on AI crawler blocking. You can do this manually by adding GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, and CCBot to your robots.txt file.
- If you use Adobe, turn on Content Credentials to source your work, and spot-check any file at [contentcredentials.org](https://contentcredentials.org/).
- Staying on Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube? Use their AI-disclosure toggles to clearly state no AI was used in your work.

#### Ongoing
- Plug into [The AI Resist List](https://airesistlist.org/), the directory of AI resistance worldwide


### Regional Resources
- **Canada:** [CARFAC](https://www.carfac.ca/) and [RAAV](https://raav.org/) (advocacy + contract guidance); [ACTRA](https://www.actra.ca/) for performers; federal AI/copyright consultation still unresolved.
- **UK:** [DACS](https://www.dacs.org.uk/) (and Payback royalties), [Association of Illustrators](https://theaoi.com/), [Equity](https://www.equity.org.uk/), [Creators' Rights Alliance](https://creatorsrightsalliance.org/); status quo maintained March 2026, personality-rights consultation promised summer 2026.
- **US:** Copyright Office AI reports; Tennessee ELVIS Act and California AB 1836/2602 likeness laws; NO FAKES Act pending.
- **EU:** GDPR gives you a genuine right to object (use it against Meta/LinkedIn); AI Act transparency obligations bite August 2026.
