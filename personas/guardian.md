# Guardian

## "F*ck AI."

### The Archetype
The Guardian sees AI as a serious threat to people and the planet. For that reason, they say no to AI. Concerned about protecting their data, they choose to opt-out wherever they can. They do not use it for work nor leisure, and they're not alone. There's many like them, who see AI as extractive and continue to find ways to say no in an ever evolving tech landscape.

### Do This

#### In minutes
- Turn off AI training in your accounts:
  - [X/Grok](https://x.com/settings/grok_settings): uncheck the training box.
  - [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/settings/data-for-ai-improvement): toggle off.
- In the EU/UK? Send Meta's [objection form](https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/510058597920541).

#### In an hour
- Block AI bots on your website:
  - **Squarespace:** Settings → Crawlers → Block known AI crawlers.
  - **On Cloudflare:** Security → Bots → Block AI training ([what's new](https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-independence-day-ai-options/)).
- Run your five most-shared images through [Glaze](https://glaze.cs.uchicago.edu/) before you post them again.

#### Ongoing
- **Connect:** [The AI Resist List](https://airesistlist.org/), a directory of AI resistance worldwide.
- **Learn:** Bookmark the [Human Data Rights Coalition opt-out guide](https://www.humandatarights.org/guides/how-to-opt-out-of-ai-training/) and re-check it every season. Platform settings change quietly.


#### In minutes

#### In an hour
- If you have your own website block AI crawlers. You can do this manually by adding a robots.txt file.
- If you use Adobe, turn on Content Credentials to source your work, and spot-check any file at [contentcredentials.org](https://contentcredentials.org/).
