# Guardian

## "F*ck AI."

### The Archetype
The Guardian sees AI as a serious threat to people and the planet. For that reason, they say no to AI. Concerned about protecting their data and all the implications of using big AI (such as the natural resource consumption and exploitative labour it costs to operate), they choose to opt-out wherever they can. They do not use it for work nor leisure, and they're not alone. There's many like them, who see AI as extractive and continue to find ways to say no in an ever evolving tech landscape.

### Do This

#### In minutes
- Turn off AI training in your accounts: Meta ([AI at Meta objection form](https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/510058597920541) — a right in the EU/UK, a request elsewhere), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6278444) (Settings → Data Privacy → Data for Generative AI Improvement → off), Grok, TikTok

#### In an hour
- If you have your own website block AI crawlers. You can do this manually by adding a robots.txt file.
- If you use Adobe, turn on Content Credentials to source your work, and spot-check any file at [contentcredentials.org](https://contentcredentials.org/).

#### Ongoing
- Plug into [The AI Resist List](https://airesistlist.org/), the directory of AI resistance worldwide
- Consider small language models (vs. LLMs) and on-prem (vs. data centres) usage to learn about new tech in a non-extractive way, while still saying no to big AI.
