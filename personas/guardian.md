# Guardian

## "F*CK AI"

### The Archetype
**The Guardian** says no to AI, for themselves and on their own terms. Concerned about protecting their data, they choose to opt out wherever they can. They do not use it for work or leisure, and they're not alone. There are many like them, who see AI as extractive and continue to find ways to say no in an ever-evolving tech landscape. Consent is personal and non-negotiable: my work, my data, my terms.

### Do This

So you're a **Guardian**, where do you go from here? Here are some ways to turn the _"F*CK AI"_ motto into action.

#### In minutes
- Turn off AI training in your accounts:
  - [X/Grok](https://x.com/settings/grok_settings): uncheck the training box.
  - [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/settings/data-for-ai-improvement): toggle off.
  - Also possible with Google, Instagram, TikTok, etc.
- In the EU/UK? Send Meta's [objection form](https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/510058597920541).

#### In an hour
- Block AI bots on your website:
  - Squarespace: Settings → Crawlers → Block known AI crawlers.
  - On Cloudflare: Security → Bots → Block AI training ([what's new](https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-independence-day-ai-options/)).
- Run your five most-shared images through [Glaze](https://glaze.cs.uchicago.edu/) before you post them again.
- Turn on Content Credentials in Adobe so your files carry proof of authorship ([contentcredentials.org](https://contentcredentials.org/)).

#### Ongoing
- Protect: keep a process archive (layered files, timestamps, process video) as proof the work is yours.
- Bookmark the [Human Data Rights Coalition opt-out guide](https://www.humandatarights.org/guides/how-to-opt-out-of-ai-training/) and re-check it every season. Platform settings change quietly.
