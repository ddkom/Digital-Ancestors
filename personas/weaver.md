# Weaver

## "Let's grow together."

### The Archetype
Weavers see new technology in a hopeful light. They see it as an opportunity to foster connections and deepen commitments to their community. They ask: can new tech help us solve big problems in the world? Weavers are critical and cautious users of AI but they find meaning in sharing with others what they learn.
