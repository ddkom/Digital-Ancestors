# Scribe

## "We draw the line together"

### The Archetype
The **Scribe** draws the lines in the sand, together with their community. They say yes only where it's fair to everyone: the mundane, and things that make art more accessible. For them the value shows when they get more time for being outdoors, making art, or being in good company. Scribes care about the environmental and socioecological implications of AI systems, and so they pick where and when to use versus not. They help their peers opt out, ask institutions hard questions, and set shared rules for when to use it and when not to.

### Do This

So you're a **Scribe**. Here's how to draw the line with others, not just for yourself.

#### In minutes
- Opt out together, not alone. Send your group chat or collective a short guide on how to opt out.
- Write yourself a one-line studio policy to give site visitors transparency into your usage.
- In [ChatGPT Data Settings](https://chatgpt.com/#settings/DataControls): turn off "Improve the model for everyone."

#### In an hour
- Automate one repeat task. Use this [Gmail → Google Calendar template](https://zapier.com/apps/gmail/integrations/google-calendar) to turn every "open call" email into a calendar deadline, for example.

#### Ongoing
- The [Green Web Foundation](https://www.thegreenwebfoundation.org/), a community working toward a fossil-free internet.
- [The AI Resist List](https://airesistlist.org/), a directory of AI resistance worldwide.
- Advocate: push for consent, credit and compensation through your artist organisation or union, such as CARFAC, RAAV, DACS or Equity.
- Speak up: respond to public consultations on copyright and AI when they open.
- Consider *small language models* (vs. LLMs) and *on-prem or on-device* (vs. data centres) usage to learn about new tech in a non-extractive way.
